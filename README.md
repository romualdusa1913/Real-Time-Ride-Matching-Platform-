## 🗺️ Using the platform

The dashboard provides a map view of your current area. 

### If you are a rider

1. Open the search bar at the top of the screen.
2. Enter your destination address.
3. Review the list of available drivers.
4. Click on a driver to see their vehicle details and current rating.
5. Select "Request Ride" to send a notification to the driver.

### If you are a driver

1. Toggle the switch to "Available" in the bottom corner.
2. Wait for a rider request to appear on your screen.
3. Review the pickup location and rider destination.
4. Click "Accept" to confirm the trip.
5. Follow the map directions to reach your passenger.
